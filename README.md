# Excel-Dashboard-from-Raw-Data-for-Europe-Bike-Store-Sales

**Overview**

This repository contains the sales analysis dashboard and dataset for Bike Europe. The Excel-based interactive dashboard (Final Sales File.xlsx) provides a comprehensive view of global sales performance, profitability, customer demographics, and product category distributions across multiple years.   

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

The dashboard tracks the following top-level metrics:

Total Quantity Sold: 13,45,316 units   
Total Cost: ₹5,30,49,908.00   
Total Profit: ₹3,22,21,100.00   
Total Revenue: ₹8,52,71,008.00   

**Dashboard Visualizations & Features**

1. Interactive Slicers / Filters

   Year: Filter by sales year (2011 – 2016).
   Country: Filter across regions (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, United Kingdom, United States).
   Age Category: Segment data by target demographic (Adults, Seniors, Young Adults, Youth).
   Product Category: Filter by primary lines (Accessories, Bikes, Clothing).

2. Country-Wise Financial Performance (Bar Chart)
  
   Visualizes Sum of Revenue vs. Sum of Profit across key geographical markets.
   Highlights top revenue-generating markets, led by the United States and Australia.

3. Revenue by Product Category & Sub-Category (Column Chart)

  Categorizes sales across Accessories, Bikes, and Clothing sub-categories.   
  Breaks down performance for sub-categories such as Mountain Bikes, Road Bikes, Touring Bikes, Helmets, Tires and Tubes, Jerseys, etc.       

4. Demographic Breakdowns
  
   Age Category Share (Pie Chart):

   Adults: 49%
   Young Adults: 32%
   Youth: 18%
   Seniors: 1%

   Gender Distribution (Pie Chart):

   Male (M): 52%
   Female (F): 48%
